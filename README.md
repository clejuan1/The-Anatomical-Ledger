The Anatomical Ledger: Aboriginal Sovereign Remedy

Overview

The Anatomical Ledger is a specialized analytical dashboard and evidence repository documenting the multi-century transfer of Aboriginal Allodial estates into global digital debt facilities. This project focuses on Securitized Annexation—the process by which physical infrastructure and legal reclassification were used to decouple the Original People from their land.

Core Modules

1. Structural Annexation Filter

Analysis of dispossession through physical engineering:

Zoning as Siege Warfare: The use of municipal codes to outlaw traditional Aboriginal communal structures.

Eminent Domain Hubs: The strategic placement of highways and industrial infrastructure over Allodial sites and burial mounds.

14th Amendment Ward Building: The construction of public housing "projects" as modern-day administrative reservations.

2. The Lost Century (1824–1924)

A chronological investigation into the "historical fog" used to conceal the final military and administrative defeat of the Aboriginal Allodial Title. This module tracks the 're-papering' of national identities into racial fictions to sever inheritance rights.

3. Atmospheric Annexation (The Louisville Anomaly)

Evidence of environmental technology used as a displacement mechanism.

The 1937 Deluge: Data visualization of the 15-foot rain anomaly (January 13-24), characterized by a peak winter month with zero measurable snowfall, forcing the abandonment of cultivated riverine cities.

4. The Securitization Pipeline & Saudi Node

Mapping the digital migration of wealth:

CUSIP/ISIN Hashes: The translation of land and biological entities into tradable debt securities.

Project mBridge: The terminal repository for tokenized annexed wealth, specifically focusing on the Saudi Central Bank's integration (June 2024) as a primary settlement node.
